Adaptive Trading System with Market Regime Detection — Bachelor Thesis (TFG)

Design, implementation and validation of an adaptive algorithmic trading system that automatically detects the prevailing market regime using two Hidden Markov Models acting as a dual orchestrator, and dynamically allocates capital across four specialized portfolio strategies. The system operates on a multi-asset universe of nine liquid ETFs (equities, treasuries, credit, gold, commodities), incorporates macro signals from the Federal Reserve (FRED) and implied volatility (VIX), and is evaluated on a strictly causal walk-forward backtest from 2012 to 2026 with realistic transaction costs.

How It Works

  • Dual-HMM Orchestrator: a Macro HMM (credit spread BAA10Y, yield-curve slope T10Y2Y) distinguishes Expansion vs. Contraction, while a Volatility HMM (VIX, realized volatilities, SPY–TLT correlation) distinguishes Low vs. High Risk. Their 2×2 intersection defines four regimes: Growth, Crash, and two Sideways states
  • Regime-Specialized Sleeves: each regime activates its own asset universe and optimization method — Max Sharpe (SLSQP, bounded) in Growth, Hierarchical Risk Parity in Crash, Risk Parity / ERC and Inverse Volatility in the Sideways regimes
  • MA200 Veto + Vol-Targeting Overlay: a trend-filter layer remaps weak Sideways days to the offensive sleeve, and a causal vol-targeting overlay caps strategy volatility at 10%, parking the remainder in short-term treasuries
  • Anti-Whipsaw Logic: monthly rebalancing, with early rebalancing only if a new regime persists 3 consecutive days — filtering spurious signals and reducing turnover

Rigorous, Causal Validation

  • Walk-Forward Training: expanding window with quarterly retraining (58 retrains), producing 3,625 consecutive out-of-sample days (2012–2026) with zero look-ahead by construction (global feature shift, train-only scaling, causal covariance estimation)
  • Economic Sanity Checks: the model flags the COVID-19 crash in real time (34.9% of days in Crash regime, 0% in Growth during Feb–May 2020) and classifies 2017 — the calmest market in recent history — as 100% Growth
  • Ablation Study: four full backtests quantify each signal's contribution — removing the credit spread collapses Sharpe from 0.765 to 0.136; removing the SPY–TLT correlation worsens max drawdown from −20.7% to −26.0% (a pure tail-protection signal)
  • Realistic Frictions: 10 bps transaction cost per unit of turnover, applied daily, with identically-costed benchmarks

Results (2012–2026, out-of-sample)

  • Final system: Sharpe 0.875 vs. 0.813 for SPY Buy & Hold and 0.738 for the institutional 60/40 benchmark
  • Max drawdown −20.6% vs. −33.7% for SPY and −27.7% for 60/40 — with roughly half of SPY's volatility (8.1% vs. 16.6%)
  • Consistent performance across subperiods (Sharpe ≈ 0.90 in both 2012–2018 and 2019–2026)
  • Core finding: regime intelligence improves risk management without sacrificing risk-adjusted return — positive returns across all four regimes, including crashes
